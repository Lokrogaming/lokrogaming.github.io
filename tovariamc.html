Server Rules

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players with respect. Harassment, racism, sexism, or hate speech will result in an immediate ban.

02

No Cheating

X-ray, flying, auto-clickers, or any third-party clients that provide an unfair advantage are strictly forbidden.

03

No Griefing

Do not destroy or modify other players' builds without permission. We value creativity and effort.

04

No Spamming

Keep the chat clean. Avoid excessive caps, repeated messages, or advertising other servers.

05

Fair Economy

Do not exploit bugs or glitches to gain items or currency. Report any bugs to the staff immediately.

06

Staff Decisions

Staff members have the final say in disputes. If you feel a decision was unfair, appeal on our Discord.
