MARK

Logo / Wordmark

Two variants: light on dark, dark on light. Keep clearance, never distort.

On dark · lokro-white.png
On light · lokro-black.png
Usage
  • ·Leave enough space around the logo — at least the height of the wordmark.
  • ·Do not squash, stretch or rotate.
  • ·No unauthorized colors or filters.
  • ·No extra shadows outside the intended design.
