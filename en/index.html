WHY LOKRO

Access is not a luxury.

Much of today's web charges subscription fees, demands ad tracking, or hides behind paywalls. LOKRO takes a different path: we build tools, games and communities that everyone can use — regardless of budget.

Every project starts from a real community need and is developed from scratch: from infrastructure to the last feature. No project is too small to keep free.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Principles over paywall.

Six simple rules define how LOKRO builds, decides and releases.

01 · NON-PROFIT

100% non-profit

No investors, no sale, no profit targets. Everything we create stays freely accessible.

02 · ACCESS

Free for everyone

No subscription, no paywall, no hidden costs — usable regardless of budget.

03 · COMMUNITY

Community first

Projects grow from real LokroGang needs — feedback sets the direction.

04 · SELF-BUILT

Self-developed

From infrastructure to feature: we build it ourselves instead of just piecing things together.

05 · OPENNESS

Honest & direct

Clear communication on Discord and GitHub — no marketing speak, no empty promises.

06 · ACTION

Rather ship it

Start small, share fast, improve together. That's how over 100 subprojects grew.
